What AIRGUARD Does

Built around your personal data.

Unlike generic health apps, every insight AIRGUARD surfaces comes from comparing today's environment with your own logged history — not a population average.

Personal baseline, not a global average

AIRGUARD compares today's conditions against your own 30-day personal history — not an arbitrary benchmark that doesn't apply to you.

Real-time environment overlay

See your current AQI, temperature, UV index, humidity, and pollen in one clear view — alongside what they've meant for you historically.

Calm, 30-second check-ins

Log your energy, comfort, symptoms, and a quick note in a quiet, focused form. No confusing grids or overwhelming choices.

AI-powered pattern summaries

AIRGUARD AI reads across your entire history and explains what it's noticed — in plain language, without jargon or alarmist framing.

Fully local and encrypted

Your check-ins live in your browser's sandboxed storage, encrypted with AES-256. Nothing is uploaded, no account required to start.

Timeline view that tells a story

A 30-day overlay chart shows exactly where your environment and wellness scores moved together — making patterns visible at a glance.

How It Works

Four steps to your first pattern.

1
Set your location
Tell AIRGUARD where you are. It reads real-time air quality, weather, and UV data for your city.
2
Log how you feel
Do a quick daily check-in — energy, comfort, any symptoms, and an optional note. Takes under 30 seconds.
3
Build your history
After 7–14 days, AIRGUARD starts finding correlations between conditions and how you felt on those days.
4
See your patterns
AIRGUARD surfaces your personal environmental triggers with confidence scores and plain-language summaries.
